If you contact us by email, we process the information you provide, particularly your email address, the content of your message and any other contact details you provide, in order to handle your enquiry.

If your message concerns steps prior to entering into a contract or the performance of a contract, the processing is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Other business enquiries are processed on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the proper handling and response to your enquiry.

According to the reviewed technical configuration, this website does not set its own cookies and does not access your browser's Local Storage or Session Storage. It uses no analytics tools, advertising pixels or comparable tracking technologies. No consent banner is therefore used.

If non-essential cookies, storage access, analytics, advertising or embedded services are introduced in the future, this notice will be updated before they are activated and consent will be obtained where required.

5. Forms, newsletters, accounts and embedded content

According to the reviewed technical configuration, the website contains no contact form, newsletter subscription, user accounts or checkout. It embeds no Instagram feeds, YouTube players, maps, external fonts or other third-party content.

The website is provided through HTTPS. This encrypts data while it is transmitted between your browser and the website. Absolute protection against every risk cannot be guaranteed.
